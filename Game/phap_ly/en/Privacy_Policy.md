## 1. Who we are

## 2. In short

- The Game works **offline**. We **do not ask** for your name, email, phone number, date of birth, photos, location, contacts or any other personal identifiers.
- We only process **game data**: progress, Virtual Items and settings — stored **on your device**.
- **We do not operate our own servers** and do not collect this data ourselves.
- Some **third-party services** (Google, Apple, ad partners) may process data under their own policies — see Section 4.
- We **do not sell** your data.

## 4. Third-party services

## 5. Children

5.1. The Game is intended for players aged **10+**. We **do not knowingly collect** personal information from children; the Game has no chat, friends, sharing or personal-data entry features.

5.2. For users under 13 (or the age set by local law), ads are **non-personalised only** and served by partners certified for children's audiences.

5.3. Parents may contact **tiepdv00@gmail.com** with questions, or delete all data as described in Section 7.

## 6. Retention

Data on your device remains until you delete the Game or use "Delete data". Backups held by Google/Apple are kept according to your account settings with those platforms.

## 7. Your rights

- **View, edit**: progress, name and avatar are shown directly in the Game.
- **Delete**: Settings → Data & purchases → **Delete data** (deletes device data and the linked cloud save). You can also remove backups in Google Play Games / iCloud settings.
- **Withdraw consent / unlink**: in your platform account settings; disable personalised ads in your device privacy settings.
- Users in the EU/UK have additional GDPR rights (access, objection, complaint to a data protection authority). Users in Vietnam have rights under Decree 13/2023/ND-CP on personal data protection [counsel to confirm applicability].

Because we do not store data on servers, most requests are carried out directly on your device.

## 8. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device and in your Google/Apple account. Protect your device and accounts with a passcode or password.

## 9. International transfers

Partners (Google, Apple, ad networks) may process data on servers outside Vietnam under their own policies.
