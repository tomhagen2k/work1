## 2. Payment

- Payments go through **Google Play** or the **Apple App Store**; prices follow the store's price tiers, currency and taxes in your country.
- Purchases are delivered **as soon as the store confirms** the transaction. If you were charged but did not receive the item: reopen the Game (online) or tap Restore purchases; if it is still missing, email the **order number** (from your Google/Apple receipt) to **tiepdv00@gmail.com**.

## 3. Minors

Players under 18 need a parent's or guardian's permission before buying. Parents should enable **purchase authentication** or **"Ask to Buy"** (Family Link / Family Sharing) to approve each purchase.

## 4. Refunds

4.1. Because payments are processed by the app stores, **refund requests go directly to the store** under its policy:
- Google Play: [play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) → select the order → Request a refund. Under Google Play's policy, refunds may be requested within a short period after purchase.
- Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) → select the order → Request a refund.

4.2. We **do not issue refunds directly** but will help confirm details for the store when you contact us.

4.3. When a transaction is refunded, **the corresponding Virtual Items are removed** from the Game (which may make your diamond balance negative or remove a skin). Refund abuse is prohibited (Terms Section 7).

4.4. If a technical fault causes loss of purchased items (not caused by you deleting data or not backing up), contact us by email and we will consider restoring the Virtual Items.

4.5. This policy does not limit your rights under consumer protection laws where you live.
